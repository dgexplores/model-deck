What is happening under the hood?
A standardized logistic regression, random forest, and small two-hidden-layer neural network are trained offline. The service verifies each artifact against its SHA-256 manifest, validates incoming batches, and records aggregate timing in SQLite. Training and model uploads are deliberately absent from the public API.
